## Sepsis-3 definitions predict ICU mortality in a low-middle-income country

**Study Title:** Sepsis-3 definitions predict ICU mortality in a low-middle-income country  
**Authors:** Bruno Adler Maccagnan Pinheiro Besen; Thiago Gomes Romano; Antonio Paulo Nassar Jr.; Leandro Utino Taniguchi; Luciano Cesar Pontes Azevedo; Pedro Vitale Mendes; Fernando Godinho Zampieri; Marcelo Park  
**Year:** 2016  
**Journal:** Not reported  
**Doi:** Not reported  
**Keywords:** Infection; Intensive care unit; Sepsis; Septic shock; Lactate; Organ dysfunction  
**Population:** Patients diagnosed with sepsis admitted to a public university ICU in Brazil  
**Sample Size:** N=957  
**Predictor / Phenotyping Approach:** Sepsis-3 definitions and lactate levels  
**Outcome:** ICU mortality  
**Timing:** First day of ICU admission  
**Method:** Retrospective cohort study  
**Effect Size:** Not reported  
**Performance / Outcomes:** ICU mortality rate: 32%  
**Notes:** Study conducted in a Brazilian public ICU; Sepsis-3 definitions were validated in a low-middle-income country setting.  
**Summary:** This study evaluates the accuracy of Sepsis-3 definitions in predicting ICU mortality compared to Sepsis-2 definitions in a Brazilian ICU, highlighting the role of lactate levels in mortality stratification.  
**Source:** Not reported

## A risk score based on pediatric sequential organ failure assessment predicts 90-day mortality in children with Klebsiella pneumoniae bloodstream infection

**Study Title:** A risk score based on pediatric sequential organ failure assessment predicts 90-day mortality in children with Klebsiella pneumoniae bloodstream infection  
**Authors:** Shuang Li; Jingxian Liu; Feng Chen; Kang Cai; Jintong Tan; Wei Xie; Rong Qian; Xiaoqin Liu; Wenhong Zhang; Huimin Du; Ying Liu; Lisu Huang  
**Year:** 2020  
**Journal:** BMC Infectious Diseases  
**Doi:** 10.1186/s12879-020-05156-5  
**Keywords:** Klebsiella pneumoniae; Children; Mortality; Risk score  
**Population:** Pediatric patients diagnosed with Klebsiella pneumoniae bloodstream infection  
**Sample Size:** N=146  
**Predictor / Phenotyping Approach:** Pediatric Sequential Organ Failure Assessment (SOFA)  
**Outcome:** 90-day mortality  
**Timing:** At the onset of Kp-BSI  
**Method:** Retrospective cohort study; Cox proportional hazard model; Receiver operating characteristic curves  
**Effect Size:** AUC of SOFA = 0.80; AUC of modified SOFA = 0.89  
**Performance / Outcomes:** Sensitivity = 0.86; Specificity = 0.84  
**Notes:** Not reported  
**Summary:** The modified SOFA score is a better predictor of 90-day mortality in pediatric patients with Klebsiella pneumoniae bloodstream infection compared to the original SOFA score.  
**Source:** Abstract, Methods, Results
